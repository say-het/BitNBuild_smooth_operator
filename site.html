Architecture Deep-Dive

The 7 Subsystems of ResQ AI

1

Canonical Event Pipeline

Multi-source adapters ingest 911 audio transcripts, citizen photo reports, IoT sensor thresholds, and social streams. Produces immutable canonical events with deterministic idempotency keys and transactional DB commits. 

POST /api/v1/events
POST /api/v1/events/citizen-report
2

AI & Spatio-Temporal Correlation

Gemini parses unstructured text/images into schema-restricted IncidentCandidate objects. PostGIS spatio-temporal clustering fuses signals within 500m & 30-minute windows. 

gemini-2.5-flash (Structured Output)
PostGIS ST_DWithin + ST_Distance
3

Resource Optimizer (OR-Tools)

CP-SAT constraint optimization sidecar calculates optimal resource allocation matching vehicle capabilities (Fire, Hazmat, EMS, Heavy Rescue) with incident severity, travel times, and hospital beds. 

POST /api/v1/optimization/recommend
fallback: FALLBACK_GREEDY
4

Response Orchestrator

Executes single & bulk dispatches inside atomic database transactions. Atomic conditional resource claims and partial unique active-assignment indexes strictly prevent double assignments under race conditions. 

POST /incidents/:id/assignments
POST /assignments/bulk
5

OSRM Routing & Road Hazards

Computes real road-network turn-by-turn routes, ETA geometry, and integrates real-time road hazard reports (e.g. collapsed bridges, flooded tunnels) to issue route warnings and reassignments. 

POST /api/v1/geo/route
GET /api/v1/geo/roads
6

Realtime Fan-Out & Rooms

Centralized Socket.IO transport multiplexed into scoped rooms (operations, alerts, incident:{id}). Backed by Redis adapter for horizontal scaling across nodes. 

ws://localhost:4000/socket.io
Redis Socket.IO Adapter
Core Architecture Principle

AI vs. Deterministic Authority Boundary

Strict separation of probabilistic & stateful logic
✨

Probabilistic AI Domain (Advisory & Interpretation)

  • ✓ Incident Intelligence: Extracts entities, casualties, and hazards from free-text and citizen photos.
  • ✓ Situation Analyst: Synthesizes cross-agency operational summaries, risks, and resource gap warnings.
  • ✓ Command Copilot: Answers operator questions using bounded read-only operational tools.
  • ✗ CANNOT: Directly mutate state, assign resources, bypass validation, or execute dispatches.
⚙️

Deterministic Core Domain (Authoritative State)

  • ✓ Spatio-Temporal Correlation: Strict mathematical PostGIS radius & time-window incident grouping.
  • ✓ Response Orchestrator: Transactional state machine with atomic DB locks preventing race conditions.
  • ✓ Monitoring Engine: SLA timers, stalled stage triggers, ETA delay calculations, and alert deduplication.
  • ✓ PostgreSQL/PostGIS: The single source of operational truth across all services.
State Machines

Incident & Assignment Lifecycles

🚨 Incident Operational State Machine

REPORTED Initial candidate or sensor trigger
↓
ASSESSING Optimizer evaluates required capabilities
↓
DISPATCHED Resources assigned and confirmed
↓
ON_SCENE First unit arrives on site
↓
RESOLVED / CANCELLED Post-incident audit & unit release

🚑 Unit Assignment Lifecycle

ASSIGNED Unit reserved via conditional claim
↓
EN_ROUTE OSRM dynamic route navigation active
↓
ON_SCENE On-site mitigation in progress
↓
COMPLETED Unit restored to AVAILABLE status
